Original Snow to Desert theater by Vesa Piittinen. I finished what Vesa started by making the missing shore lines. I also had to remove the bridges and rivers that were coverted as they were not the same shape as bridges and rivers that RA1 uses. Becuase of this I remade the bridges and rivers so that they would fit the existing maps. I also changed the Tiberium back to Ore. 

All of the civilian buildings have been change to those used in C&C 95 desert maps. Tress from Tiberian Sun have been used to replace the snow tress.
